Frequently Asked

Common Skill to Profit questions.

Is Skill to Profit legit?
Yes. Skill to Profit is the flagship coaching program from The Kaizen, a UK-registered company (Kaizen LTD) founded by Callum Carver. The program has a 4.6/5 average rating from 65 verified Trustpilot reviews and has helped over 500 students collectively generate $2M+ in revenue. You can verify the company at Trustpilot, Whop, and Crunchbase.
Is Skill to Profit the same as skills-profit.com?
No. They are completely unrelated. Skill to Profit (no hyphen, at skilltoprofit.co) is a coaching program by The Kaizen, founded by Callum Carver. Skills-profit.com (with a hyphen) is an unrelated platform we have no connection with. If you've seen scam warnings about skills-profit.com, those do not apply to Skill to Profit by The Kaizen.
How much does Skill to Profit cost?
Skill to Profit is available in two tiers: the group program ($3,900–$4,900) and 1-on-1 mentorship ($9,000–$10,000+). Specific pricing depends on payment plan and current intake. Apply through /apply for current pricing.
Does Skill to Profit work for beginners?
Yes. The program is built specifically for people starting from existing skills they already have — no business experience required. Most students see their first paying client within 8–12 weeks of joining. Results depend on the student's existing skill set and execution.
Is there a refund policy?
Skill to Profit has an Action-Based Guarantee — if you complete the required program actions and don't see results, you can request a refund within the policy window. Full refund terms are provided in the contract before purchase. Read the contract carefully and ask questions of the sales team before joining.
Where can I read independent Skill to Profit reviews?
Independent reviews are available on Trustpilot (4.6/5, 65 verified reviews) and on the Whop marketplace (verified purchases). Both are independent platforms not controlled by The Kaizen.
Who runs Skill to Profit?
Skill to Profit is created and run by Callum Carver, founder of The Kaizen. Callum has been featured in Voice of London, Talk Twenties Podcast, YEARBOOK Podcast, UNBOXED, and Saulderson Media. The Kaizen team includes 15+ coaches and staff.
